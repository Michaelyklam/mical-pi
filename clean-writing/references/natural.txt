# Natural writing

Remove accidental AI patterns without flattening the writer's voice.

- Preserve deliberate rhythm, warmth, humor, emphasis, and informality.
- Prefer specific observations to generic abstractions.
- Remove repetition only when it adds no meaning or emphasis.
- Remove chatbot artifacts and empty promotional language.
- Do not impose fixed sentence-length or vocabulary limits.
- Do not categorically ban punctuation, metaphors, rhetorical questions,
  fragments, or phrasal verbs.
- Match an available voice sample before applying generic preferences.

If clarity and personality conflict, preserve the intended voice unless the
meaning becomes ambiguous.
