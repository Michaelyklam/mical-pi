# Technical writing

Prefer concrete explanation over polished-sounding abstraction.

- Use consistent terminology.
- Describe behavior before claiming benefits.
- Remove hedging that does not change the claim.
- Remove quality adjectives that are not supported by evidence.
- Split sentences when they contain unrelated claims.
- Keep necessary technical terms, qualifications, and causal explanations.
- Do not add a generic introduction or conclusion.
- Do not remove useful detail merely to make the text shorter.
