# Movie / show / music reactions

Source: `Documents/Michael's Thoughts/Entertainment` (~38 files).

## Structure
- No consistent frontmatter — an inline hashtag opens most files (`#Anime`, `#Movie`, `#Music`).
- Anime/shows follow a template: `Genres:` tags, `Lead Composer:` (wikilinked), `Date Watched:`, a headline `Personal/Overall Rating: (X of 10)`, then `###`/`####` sub-ratings (Visuals, Story, Music — each with its own number) followed by a free-prose `### Thoughts`.
- Movies use a lighter version: composer, rating, date watched, "Interesting Notes."
- Fields are often left blank (empty genres, no story score) — completion is uneven; don't force every field to be filled if there's nothing to say.
- Music notes drop the rating template entirely and become short reflective paragraphs.
- Wikilinks are dense — to composers, studios, other titles, dated notes, even abstract nouns (`[[Hope]]`, `[[Success]]`).

## Sentence & paragraph style
- Heavy first-person framing: "I watched this because...", "I mainly watched this for the soundtrack."
- Informal, comma-spliced/run-on sentences; lowercase sentence starts happen naturally.
- Comparisons to other media are the primary tool for calibration: "similar to how X felt," "not as detailed as Y," "not a tear jerker on the level of Z."
- Hedging is a signature move: "might," "to be seen," "I might change my mind after re-listening."
- Occasional rhetorical/incredulous asides break up the analysis.

## Formatting habits
- Mostly prose, not bullets — bullets appear only for the occasional ranked list ("3 Good / 3 Bad").
- Numeric scores (sometimes to a decimal — 9.8, 7.5) are the main structural device, usually with explicit rationale for the exact number.
- Bold essentially unused — headers do the emphasis work instead.

## Tone & vocabulary
- Casual, enthusiastic-but-measured, audio/hifi-nerd inflected.
- Recurring words: "pretty," "kinda," "definitely," "honestly," "freaking amazing," "eye candy."
- Criticism is direct but soft-pedaled. Personal-life tangents (career, family) sometimes fold into the reaction.

## Verbatim calibration
- "I'm not sure how it can get better than this. I'm only giving it a 9.8 so that I have room for something to be a 10 in the future." (Look Back)
- "Music was ok, nothing outstanding but it didn't suck either." (Solo Leveling S2)
- "It's not a tear jerker on the level of [[I want to eat your Pancreas]] but touching for sure." (Josee, Tiger and the Fish)

## Checklist
- [ ] A numeric rating with an actual one-line rationale for the specific number, not just "good"
- [ ] At least one comparison to another title/artist as the calibration device
- [ ] First-person framing of why he watched/listened, not just a review of the thing itself
- [ ] Leave fields blank rather than padding when there's genuinely nothing to say
