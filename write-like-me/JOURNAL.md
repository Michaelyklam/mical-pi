# Journal / personal storytelling

Source: `Documents/Michael's Thoughts/Journal` (786 dated entries, 2023–present).

## Structure
- No frontmatter. A tag line opens the entry (`#Daily`, sometimes stacked: `#Travel #Journal`, `#Story #Audio`).
- Entries through ~2025 mostly follow one loose skeleton: tag line → an empty `- [ ]` checkbox (often left unused) → a fixed header like `### Interesting Things that Happened today` → 1–3 prose paragraphs → a closing `---` rule → plain-text `creation date:`/`modification date:` metadata at the *bottom*, never the top.
- Many days are just the skeleton with no real content — that's fine, don't force padding on a quiet day.
- Length swings hard: 0–2 sentences on quiet days, 300–900 words on eventful ones (trips, hikes, retreats).
- From ~Feb 2026 on, structure loosens into dated `###` headers with `####` sub-sections by time-of-day/topic, occasional emoji-prefixed headers (`## 📖 Morning Devo`), and shorter paragraphs.
- Cross-references people, places, media, and routes constantly via wikilinks.

## Sentence & paragraph style
- Medium-length declarative sentences chained with "and," "so," "then," "but" — a run-on, conversational cadence, not tight prose.
- First person, chronological narration ("Then we...", "After that...").
- Short fragments land for emphasis or a quick close-out: "Pretty mid...", "Good night.", "Fun day."
- Older entries: one long paragraph per scene. Newer (2026-style): short 2–5 sentence paragraphs under headers.
- Self-corrections and hedged asides are common: "maybe not," "I don't know enough... but I'm concerned."

## Formatting habits
- Bullets for lists (packing lists, lessons learned, "significant moments"). `- [ ]`/`- [x]` checkboxes are a near-universal opener even when unused.
- Bold sparing, mostly in 2026-era entries for key terms/times.
- No tables. Em-dashes occasional, more common in newer entries for asides.
- Ellipses for a trailing thought ("it was pretty mid... Yea").

## Tone & vocabulary
- Casual, plainspoken, diary-like — no literary polish attempted.
- Verbal tics: "pretty [adjective]" (pretty good, pretty mid, pretty productive), "definitely," "honestly," "I realized," "I think there's a balance."
- Lessons-learned framed as an itemized recap after events, not introspective prose: "Firstly... Second thing... Third thing..."
- Faith content is sincere and unironic ("praise God," "the Lord," "faith journey") when it comes up.
- Humor is deadpan and flat: "it was pretty mid," "Never again."

## Verbatim calibration
- "We jammed on the instruments a bit... it was pretty mid... Yea I probably wouldn't buy it for myself." (2024-07-04)
- "I'm stronger than I think mostly and I can probably do without tylenol." (2025-12-01)
- "Was using the steam technique for the salt bread... it to absolutely shatter and throw glass all over the kitchen. Genuinely terrifying... Never again." (2026-02-21)

## Checklist
- [ ] First person, chronological, run-on-but-readable sentences — not polished prose
- [ ] No forced "in conclusion" — let it trail off
- [ ] Lessons/takeaways as a short itemized list if the day was eventful
- [ ] Deadpan one-liners over explained jokes
- [ ] Skip padding a quiet day into something bigger than it was
