# Work / career reflection

Source: `Documents/Michael's Thoughts/Work/Career` (~32 files — self-evaluations, "Communication in the workplace," "Interview Managers"). Not `Work/Meetings` — those are terse fragments, not representative prose.

## Structure
- No YAML frontmatter — line 1 is almost always a hashtag string used Twitter-style (`#Work #Career`, `#framework #Communication`).
- Frameworks (STAR, C.A.R., "What/Why/So What") get one `###` header per component, each with a one-line definition.
- Self-evaluations mirror the company's own prompt questions verbatim as `###` headers, then answer in theme-grouped paragraphs, often closing with a "Side Notes" subsection.
- Heavy `[[wikilinks]]` to people and related notes, and `![[embeds]]` to transclude a referenced story rather than repeat it.

## Sentence & paragraph style
- Two modes coexist:
  1. Terse, fragment-heavy notes-to-self — lowercase starts, missing punctuation, comma splices — for checklists and scratch specs.
  2. Fuller explanatory paragraphs (self-evals, STAR-style stories) — longer, run-on, comma-stacked, but still conversational, not polished.
- Imperative/second-person voice when writing advice to future-self: "Be friendly, make jokes," "admit you are out of your depth, do not fake it."
- Professional-reflective but unpolished — reads as thinking-out-loud, not final copy.

## Formatting habits
- Bullets/numbered lists dominate over prose, especially for processes and recaps.
- Numbered "recap" lists often close a narrative section, restating the process just described in 2–3 steps.
- Bold reserved for field labels in template-like docs (**Scope of issue:**), not general emphasis.
- Parenthetical asides are frequent: "(at the time of writing)," "(if applicable)."

## Tone & vocabulary
- Pragmatic, process-oriented, mildly self-critical but ultimately confident — self-evals pair an acknowledged gap with a concrete plan ("I understand there is still more to learn... I would like to work on").
- Domain jargon (recreate, root cause, escalation, workaround, triage) used fluently, without over-explaining it.

## Checklist
- [ ] Self-evals pair every acknowledged gap with a concrete next step — no gap left unaddressed
- [ ] Headers mirror the actual prompt/process being answered, not a generic template
- [ ] A short numbered recap after a longer explanatory section, if the piece is process-heavy
- [ ] Confident but not boastful — claims backed by a concrete stat or example
